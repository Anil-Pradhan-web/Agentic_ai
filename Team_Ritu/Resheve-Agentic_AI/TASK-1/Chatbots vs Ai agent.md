**CHATBOTS SCENARIOS :-**





**1.** Someone just wants to know "what time do you open?" — that's it, nothing else to do after telling them. Chatbot handles this easily.



**2.** An employee asks "how many leaves do I have left?" — we just check and tell them. No task, just an answer. Chatbot is enough here.



**3.** A visitor on the website asks "do you deliver to my area?" or "how much does this cost?" — again just a quick answer, nothing to actually do. Chatbot job.



**4.** A patient calls up asking "what's the fee for a checkup?" — simple question, simple answer, no action needed. Chatbot.



**5.** Someone asks "how do I reset my password?" — if we're just telling them the steps, that's a chatbot thing. But the moment we actually go and reset it for them, that becomes a task — and that's agent territory.







**AI AGENT SCENARIOS :-**





**1.** Let's say the goal is "match all vendor invoices with purchase orders and fix the accounting entries" — this isn't a question, it's a job with steps and an end result. Needs an agent.



**2.** If someone says "don't just tell them how to reset the password, actually reset it and close the ticket" — now it's not talk, it's work. That needs an agent.



**3.** "Go through resumes, pick the good ones, and set up interviews" — this is a proper task with multiple steps and a real outcome. Agent's job.



**4.** "If a delivery gets delayed, pull stock from another warehouse and update the customer automatically" — there's a problem to fix and action to take. That's agent work.



**5.** "Keep checking competitor prices every week and send me a report" — this keeps running and has a clear goal to hit each time. Needs an agent, not a one-time chatbot reply.
