Privacy Policies of Major LLM Providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic Claude)

OpenAI (ChatGPT) - User data may be used to improve models (depending on
account settings and plan). - Users can control chat history and
training preferences. - Enterprise and Team data is not used for model
training by default. - Conversations are protected using
industry-standard security measures. - Users can request data deletion
and export.

Google (Gemini) - Prompts may be reviewed to improve Google’s AI
services. - Data handling depends on the Google product and user
settings. - Enterprise Workspace data is generally not used to train
foundation models without permission. - Users can manage activity
through Google Account settings. - Supports data export and deletion
through Google services.

Anthropic (Claude) - Focuses on privacy and responsible AI
development. - Consumer conversations may be used to improve models
unless users opt out where available. - API customer data is not used
for training by default. - Enterprise customer data receives additional
privacy protections. - Users can request deletion of stored data
according to Anthropic’s policies.
