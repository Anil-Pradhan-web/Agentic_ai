Task:-

1.  Write 5 problem Scenarios where ChatBot is the only solution which will be beneficial

for client.
Ans:-
•

 Airline Customer Support
Problem: Airlines receive thousands of customer inquiries about flight schedules,
baggage allowances, online check-in, cancellations, and refund policies.
Solution: A chatbot can instantly answer these common questions 24/7, reducing
call center workload and helping passengers get quick information.

•  Library Management

Problem: Students frequently ask about book availability, due dates, library
timings, and fine details.

Solution: A chatbot can instantly provide library information without requiring
librarian assistance.

•  Banking Customer Support

Problem: Banks receive thousands of customer queries every day regarding
account balance, credit card issues, loan details, branch timings, and transaction
status.
Solution: A chatbot can instantly answer these frequently asked questions 24/7,
reducing customer waiting time and allowing human agents to focus on complex
banking issues.

•  University Admission Helpdesk

Problem: During the admission season, colleges receive a large number of
inquiries about eligibility criteria, application deadlines, fees, required
documents, and available courses.

Solution: A chatbot can provide accurate and instant responses to students at
any time, reducing the workload on the admission office and improving the
student experience.

•  Hospital Information Desk

Problem: Hospitals receive hundreds of daily calls regarding doctor availability,
appointment booking, OPD timings, and laboratory report collection.
Solution: A chatbot can provide this information instantly, allowing hospital staff
to spend more time on patient care rather than answering repetitive phone calls.

2.  Write 5 Problem Scenarios where Al-Agent will be the best solution for the given

problem scenario.
 Ans:-

•  Employee Onboarding Automation

Problem: HR teams spend significant time creating employee accounts, collecting
documents, assigning training, and sending welcome emails.
Solution: An AI agent can automate the complete onboarding process by
coordinating multiple systems with minimal human intervention.

•  Email Management Assistant

Problem: A manager receives hundreds of emails every day. Reading every email,
replying, and scheduling meetings takes a lot of time.
Solution: An AI agent can read emails, identify important messages, draft replies,
schedule meetings on the calendar, and send reminders automatically.

•  School Attendance and Parent Notification

Problem: Teachers spend time marking attendance and informing parents when
students are absent.
Solution: An AI agent can record attendance, identify absent students, send SMS
or email notifications to parents, and prepare attendance reports automatically.

•  Smart Agriculture

Problem: Farmers need to monitor soil moisture, weather conditions, irrigation
schedules, and crop health to maximize productivity.
Solution: An AI agent can analyze data from sensors and weather forecasts,
automatically schedule irrigation, recommend fertilizer usage, and alert farmers
about potential crop diseases.

•  Cybersecurity Threat Detection

Problem: Organizations must continuously monitor networks for suspicious
activities and cyberattacks.
Solution: An AI agent can detect threats in real time, isolate affected systems,
alert security teams, and generate incident reports automatically.
