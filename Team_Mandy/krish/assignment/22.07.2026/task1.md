# AI Platforms: Security, Privacy, and Data Management

## OpenAI

OpenAI provides AI-powered services such as ChatGPT, the OpenAI API, ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, Codex, and developer tools. Protecting customer information is a fundamental part of its security and privacy strategy.

### Security and Access Control
OpenAI secures customer data using encryption during transmission and while stored. Access to user information is restricted to authorized employees under strict access control policies, audit logging, confidentiality agreements, and regular security training. Enterprise, Business, and API customer data is not used for model training unless customers explicitly choose to participate.

### Privacy Controls
Users can manage their privacy through ChatGPT settings by:
- Turning chat history and model training on or off.
- Using Temporary Chat, where conversations are not saved permanently.
- Deleting individual conversations or the entire account.
- Requesting access to or deletion of personal data.

### Data Management
Depending on user settings, OpenAI may use consumer conversations and uploaded content to improve AI models. However, API and Enterprise customer data remains private and is excluded from model training by default. OpenAI also states that it does not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

### Data Deletion
Users may remove chats or permanently delete their accounts whenever they choose. Deleted conversations are removed from the account immediately and are generally erased from OpenAI's systems within 30 days, except where longer retention is legally required.

## Anthropic

Anthropic develops AI products including Claude, Claude Pro, Claude for Work, Claude Enterprise, and the Claude API. The company focuses on responsible AI development while maintaining strong privacy protections.

### Security and Access Control
Anthropic protects user information through encryption, strict authentication mechanisms, and controlled employee access. Only authorized personnel may access customer information for security, customer support, or legal compliance. Enterprise customers can configure customized data retention policies.

### Privacy Controls
Claude users can:
- Delete conversations whenever needed.
- Choose whether chats contribute to future model improvements.
- Configure retention settings in Enterprise editions.
- Revoke shared conversation links.

### Data Management
Consumer users may allow or disable the use of conversations for model improvement. Enterprise and API customer data is not used for training by default. Anthropic may retain anonymized information for research purposes where permitted by its privacy policy.

### Data Deletion
Deleted conversations disappear immediately from the user's history and are typically removed from Anthropic's backend systems within 30 days. Certain records may be retained longer for legal, security, or policy enforcement purposes.

## Google

Google offers a wide range of services including Search, Gmail, Drive, Photos, YouTube, Maps, Android, Chrome, and Google Cloud. Because of its extensive ecosystem, privacy and security play an essential role in its infrastructure.

### Security and Access Control
Google protects user information using encryption, secure cloud infrastructure, continuous monitoring, and strict internal access controls. Additional security features include Two-Step Verification, Security Checkup, Advanced Protection, Safe Browsing, and passkey authentication.

### Privacy Controls
Google provides several tools that allow users to manage their personal information, including:
- Privacy Checkup
- My Activity
- Google Account Dashboard

Users can:
- Manage Web & App Activity.
- Control Location History.
- Review YouTube watch and search history.
- Adjust advertisement personalization.
- Automatically delete activity after selected time periods.
- Browse privately using Chrome Incognito Mode.
- Review permissions granted to third-party applications.

### Data Management
Google collects information to improve services, personalize experiences, strengthen security, and enhance product performance. This may include search activity, device information, location data (when enabled), and service usage. Google states that it does not sell personally identifiable information to advertisers, and content stored in Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Photos is not used for personalized advertising.

### Data Deletion
Users can delete specific activities, selected datasets, or their entire Google Account. Activity can also be removed automatically after a chosen retention period. Once a deletion request is made, the data is removed from the account immediately, while permanent removal from backup systems may take additional time due to operational and legal requirements.
