# LLM and SLM — Why Training LLMs is Difficult for Individual Researchers

## Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models are neural networks trained on massive text corpora to perform a wide variety of natural language processing tasks.

**Characteristics:**
- Typically contain 7 billion to hundreds of billions of parameters
- Trained using trillions of tokens collected from books, websites, research papers, and other textual sources
- Require distributed training across multiple GPUs or TPUs
- Deliver high accuracy and strong generalization across diverse tasks

**Examples:**
- Llama series
- Qwen series
- GPT family
- Gemma
- Mistral

---

## Small Language Models (SLMs)

Small Language Models are compact neural networks designed to provide efficient language understanding while requiring significantly fewer computational resources.

**Characteristics:**
- Usually contain millions to a few billion parameters
- Faster inference
- Lower memory consumption
- Suitable for edge devices and resource-constrained environments
- Easier to fine-tune for domain-specific applications

**Examples:**
- TinyLlama
- Phi series
- DistilGPT models

---

## Why Training LLMs is Difficult for Individual Researchers

Although open-source LLMs are publicly available, training an LLM from scratch is generally beyond the reach of individual students, hobbyists, or small organizations. Several factors contribute to this challenge:

- Massive computational requirements
- Extremely high training cost
- Massive training datasets
- Long training time
