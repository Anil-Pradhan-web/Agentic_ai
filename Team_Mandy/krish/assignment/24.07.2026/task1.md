# What is the difference between Closed-Source Model and Open-Source and Open-Weight Model

Closed-source models are proprietary to the company that have developed it. Users can access them through APIs or applications, but cannot see or modify how they are built.

**Some features of Closed-Source models are:**
- High Performance
- Regular Support
- Vendor lock-in

**Some examples:**
- GPT 5.5 (OpenAI)
- Claude (Anthropic)
- Gemini (Google)

---

Open-source models are generally the models that provide the complete project.

**Some features are:**
- Complete Transparency
- Good for research
- Easy to modify

**Some examples:**
- GPT-NeoX
- BLOOM

---

Open-Weight Models are those models whose only trained weights are released.

**Some features:**
- Easy to deploy locally
- Supports fine-tuning
- Lower cost

**Some examples:**
- Llama 3
- Gemma
- Mistral
