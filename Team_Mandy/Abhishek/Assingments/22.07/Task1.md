OpenAI
OpenAI provides AI services such as ChatGPT, the API platform, ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, Codex, and developer tools, making the protection of user data a core part of its security and privacy framework.

Security and Access Control
OpenAI protects user data using encryption both in transit and at rest. Access to customer content is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis and is governed by strict access controls, logging, confidentiality obligations, and security training. Business offerings such as ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT Business, and the API do not use customer data for model training by default.

User Privacy Controls
Users can manage their privacy through ChatGPT settings by:
->Enabling or disabling chat history and model training (cons-.umer accounts).
->Using Temporary Chat, where conversations are not retained in chat history and are automatically deleted after a limited retention period.
->Deleting individual conversations or their entire account.
->Submitting privacy requests to access or delete personal data.

Data Management
For consumer services, OpenAI may use conversations, uploaded files, and model interactions to improve its models, depending on the user's settings. Users can opt out of training through privacy controls. For business products and the API, customer inputs and outputs are not used for model training unless the organisation explicitly opts in. OpenAI states that it does not sell personal data to advertisers.

Data Deletion
Users can delete individual chats or their entire account at any time. Deleted chats are removed from the user's account immediately and are generally scheduled for permanent deletion from OpenAI's systems within 30 days, unless legal, security, or compliance obligations require longer retention. Temporary Chats are also automatically deleted within this period.



Anthropic
Anthropic develops Claude AI, Claude Pro, Claude for Work, Claude Enterprise, and the Anthropic API. The company emphasises responsible AI development while providing privacy controls for both consumer and enterprise users.

Security and Access Control
Anthropic protects customer data through encryption, controlled internal access, and security policies. Access to user information is limited to authorised personnel for purposes such as security, customer support, legal compliance, and service improvement. Enterprise customers can configure custom data retention policies, including organisation-specific retention periods.

User Privacy Controls
Claude users can:
->Delete conversations at any time.
->Control whether their chats are used to improve Claude (consumer accounts).
->Configure retention settings in Claude Enterprise.
->Manage shared conversations and revoke public sharing links when applicable.

Data Management
Anthropic allows consumer users to choose whether their conversations may be used to improve future Claude models. For commercial offerings, including Claude for Work, Claude Enterprise, and the Anthropic API, customer data is generally not used for model training by default. Anthropic may retain de-identified data for research and service improvement where users have provided permission or where permitted by its privacy policy.

Data Deletion
Users may delete conversations at any time. Deleted chats are removed immediately from the user's conversation history and are generally deleted from Anthropic's backend systems within 30 days. Certain information related to policy violations, legal obligations, or user-submitted feedback may be retained for longer periods as described in Anthropic's Privacy Center


Google
Google operates a broad ecosystem of products and services, including Google Search, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, YouTube, Google Maps, Android, Chrome, and Google Cloud, making data privacy and security essential components of its service architecture.

Security and Access Control
Google protects customer data through encryption in transit and at rest, secure data centers, and continuous security monitoring. Access to user information is restricted to authorised personnel based on the principle of least privilege and is governed by strict confidentiality agreements and internal security policies. Additional security features such as 2-Step Verification, Security Checkup, Advanced Protection Program, Safe Browsing, and passkeys help safeguard user accounts from unauthorized access.

User Privacy Controls
Google provides comprehensive privacy management tools through Privacy Checkup, My Activity, and the Google Account Dashboard. Users can:
->View and manage Web & App Activity.
->Control Location History and Timeline.
->Manage YouTube Search and Watch History.
->Configure ad personalization preferences.
->Automatically delete activity after 3, 18, or 36 months.
->Use Chrome Incognito Mode to browse without saving local browsing history.
->Review and manage permissions granted to third-party applications.
These controls allow users to customize how their personal data is collected, stored, and used across Google's services.

Data Management
Google collects data to provide, maintain, and improve its services, personalize user experiences, enhance security, and support product development. Data collected may include search queries, device information, location data (when enabled), usage activity, and service interactions. Google states that it does not sell personally identifiable information to advertisers. Additionally, content stored in private services such as Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Photos is not used for personalized advertising. Google also anonymizes or aggregates certain datasets to improve services while reducing the identifiability of individual users.

Data Deletion
Users can delete individual activities, selected data categories, or their entire Google Account through their account settings. Google also provides automatic deletion options for activity history. Once a deletion request is submitted, the information is removed from the user's account immediately, while complete deletion from Google's distributed backup systems may require additional time. Some data may be retained temporarily for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security purposes, or disaster recovery, after which it is permanently deleted or anonymized in accordance with Google's data retention policies.
