(Usage of ChatBot and AIagents)

When to use a Chatbot (think simple, repetitive tasks):
Customer Support: Perfect for answering FAQs like hours, shipping, or returns without bugging your support team.
School Admissions: Great for handling standard inquiries about eligibility, deadlines, and requirements during busy admissions season.
Banking Info: Handles quick questions about interest rates, loans, or account types based on set policies.
Hospital Admin: Makes booking appointments and finding doctor info easy for patients, freeing up staff.
Online Shopping: Helps shoppers browse products, compare filters, and find what they need, making the experience smoother.


When to use an AI Agent (think complex, multi-step, autonomous tasks):
Travel Planning: Can handle the whole process—flights, hotels, and itineraries—and adapts if plans change.
IT Support: Independently troubleshoots software issues, manages tickets, and monitors systems without needing human oversight.
Supply Chain: Keeps an eye on inventory, predicts shortages, and makes decisions on ordering stock automatically.
Financial Portfolio: Monitors market changes and rebalances investments based on strategies, not just answering questions.
Healthcare Coordination: Manages chronic care by linking up appointments, meds, and doctor consults to keep everything on track.
